# PrivacyInspector — Privacy Policy

This repository hosts the privacy policy for the **PrivacyInspector** Android app ([com.adinspector.app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.adinspector.app)).

**Privacy Policy URL:** https://michelelaporta.github.io/privacyinspector-policy/

## About PrivacyInspector

PrivacyInspector is an Android developer tool that reveals how apps on your device collect and share data — which ad SDKs they integrate, what permissions they hold, and whether they form dangerous data-collection combinations.

- Detects 56+ ad network SDKs across all installed apps
- Optional Exodus Privacy integration
- Privacy Scanner: Privacy Sandbox, background location, GAID access, risk combinations
- All analysis runs locally — no data leaves your device

## Privacy Summary

PrivacyInspector does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. See the full policy at the URL above.
