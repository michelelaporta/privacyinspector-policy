PrivacyInspector
Privacy Policy · Effective date: November 17, 2025
PrivacyInspector does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All analysis runs entirely on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

PrivacyInspector does not collect, record, or transmit any of the following:

  • Your Advertising ID (GAID) or any device identifier
  • The list of apps installed on your device
  • Your location
  • Any personal information
  • Usage statistics or analytics

2. Local Processing Only

All scanning and analysis performed by PrivacyInspector — including ad SDK detection, permission inspection, and the Privacy Scanner — runs entirely on your device using the Android PackageManager API. No data leaves your device as a result of these features.

3. GAID History

Your Advertising ID history is stored exclusively in your device's local shared preferences (private app storage). This data is never transmitted anywhere and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. Exodus Privacy Integration

When you tap the "Enrich with Exodus" button, the app sends HTTP requests to exodus-privacy.eu.org — a third-party, non-profit tracker database — containing only the package names of apps being looked up. No personal data is included in these requests. This feature is entirely optional and only activated on user action. Please refer to the Exodus Privacy privacy policy for information about how they handle these requests.

5. Advertising

PrivacyInspector displays a banner advertisement provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your Advertising ID to serve personalised ads based on your interests. You can opt out at any time by enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), or by resetting your Advertising ID using the Reset ID button in this app.

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use AdMob, see: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Permissions Used by This App

  • AD_ID — Required to read the Advertising ID from Google Play Services and display it in the app.
  • INTERNET — Required for the AdMob banner ad and the optional Exodus Privacy API calls.
  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES — Required to enumerate installed apps for ad SDK detection, permission inspection, and the Privacy Scanner. The resulting data is processed locally and never transmitted.

7. Children's Privacy

PrivacyInspector is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the Play Store developer page.
